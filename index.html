The Detour & The Grace

Our story began beautifully, as our friendship grew quickly. But then came the tough times — everything seemed to halt. We both went our separate ways, thinking it simply wasn’t meant to be.

But as life happened, GOD also happened. Somehow, we just couldn’t picture a life without each other. Gracefully, we found our way back after months of mere cordial hellos.

One of the greatest blessings of our relationship is this genuine, deep friendship we share and are so grateful for.

Our Calling

Beyond our love for one another, we share a deep sense of purpose. We believe we are called together to reach, rescue, and deliver teenagers and young adults from darkness into God’s brilliant light. We look forward to the beautiful day where we will say our “yeses” in front of God and the amazing people in our lives, stepping forward into this shared mission hand-in-hand.

Questions

Q&A

Is it mandatory to RSVP?

Yes, please! Access on the day will be managed with the guest list, and your RSVP helps us plan seating and catering properly.

Are plus ones or kids allowed?

We love your little ones, but we kindly ask that only guests named on the invitation attend. Reach out to us if you'd like to ask about a plus one.

What is the preferred contact for questions?

Please reach the wedding planner via the email in the footer, or message the couple directly.

What colours are the guests wearing?

The colours of the day will be confirmed along with the venue details — check the Schedule section closer to the day.

What's the dress code?

Formal and fabulous. Details will be announced with the dress theme.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We kindly ask for an unplugged ceremony — our photographers will capture everything. Feel free to snap away at the reception, and tag #OfGraceAndLove!
